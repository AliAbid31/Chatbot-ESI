## About ESI
The National School of Computer Science (ESI), formerly known as the National Institute of Computer Science (INI), is an Algerian higher education institution that trains State engineers in computer science. It is located in Oued Smar, about 15 km from the city center of the capital, Algiers, in Algeria.

Founded as the Center for Computer Science Studies and Research (CERI) under the supervision of the former Ministry of Planning and Regional Development, it was renamed and placed under the supervision of the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research in 1984.

CERI was the first specialized computer science training center in Africa. It trained the first State engineers, application engineers, and programmers in Algeria and other African countries (Benin, Cameroon, Gabon, Mali, Mauritania, Senegal, Tunisia, etc.).

About concours: In the second year, each school (ESI, ESI SBA, ESTIN) organizes a competition, also known as the 2CP exam or concours, for access to the second cycle (1CS). Every year, the Ministry of Higher Education determines the number of available spots for the second cycle. The top 80% of students in the ranking are directly admitted to the second cycle without having to take the exam. The remaining 20% of places are awarded to students who participate in the written exam, based on their ranking. For example, at the end of the second year at ESI Algiers, students have two possibilities: 1. If their ranking is within the top 80%, they are directly admitted to the second cycle without having to sit the exam. 2. If their ranking is below the top 80%, they must take the written exam with students from ESI Algiers, ESI SBA, and ESTIN to compete for one of the remaining 20% of spots. For the 2024/2025 season, out of 262 students in total, 209 students from ESI Algiers were directly admitted to the second cycle, with the cutoff grade being 11.33. Students who scored between 10.00 and 11.32 were eligible to take the concours as a second chance to pass into the next year’s 1CS. Students who scored below 10.00 failed the year. Out of those who took the concours, 37 students from ESI succeeded, while the others were transferred to other universities or schools. For the 2023/2024 season, the cutoff grade for access to the concours was slightly lower, stopping at 11.23. ## Complete 5-Year Program Structure

The National Higher School of Computing graduates State Engineers in Informatics. The training is for five years split into:

### PHASE 1: TWO YEARS OF PREPARATORY CLASSES (CP)

#### First Year Preparatory Cycle (1CP)
**First Semester:**
- ALSDS (Algorithms and Static Data Structures): Fundamental concepts of algorithms and data structures
- ARCH1 (Computer Architecture 1): Basic logic circuits, Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential circuits
- ANA1 (Analysis 1): Theoretical mathematical foundations
- ALG1 (Algebra 1): Mathematical foundations for algebraic concepts
- ELECT (Electricity): Essential principles of electricity
- SYS1 (Introduction to Operating Systems 1): Basic operating system concepts, Linux environment
- TEE (Techniques of Written Expression): Writing and communication skills (French)
- BWEB (Web Basics): Introduction to web development fundamentals

**Second Semester:**
- ALSDD (Algorithms and Dynamic Data Structures): Advanced algorithmic concepts with dynamic data structures
- SYST2 (Introduction to Operating Systems 2): Continued OS study and assembly language
- ANA2 (Analysis 2): Extended mathematical concepts
- ALG2 (Algebra 2): Advanced algebraic theories
- MECA (Mechanics of Point Mass): Fundamental principles of classical mechanics
- ELECF1 (Fundamental Electronics 1): Basic electronics concepts and circuit applications
- TEO (Oral Techniques): Oral communication skills development
- ANG1 (English 1): Computer-related English vocabulary

#### Second Year Preparatory Cycle (2CP)
**First Semester:**
- ECON (Business Economics): Introduction to economic principles and business concepts
- ANA3 (Mathematical Analysis 3): Advanced mathematical analysis concepts
- ARCHI2 (Computer Architecture 2): Advanced computer architecture topics
- ELECF2 (Fundamental Electronics 2): Advanced electronics concepts and applications
- ANG2 (English 2): Continued development of technical English skills
- ALG3 (Algebra 3): Advanced algebraic concepts and applications
- SFSD (File Structures and Data Structures): File organization and advanced data structure concepts
- PRST1 (Probability and Statistics 1): Introduction to probability theory and statistical methods

**Second Semester:**
- ANG3 (English 3): Advanced technical English communication
- LOGM (Mathematical Logic): Formal logic systems and mathematical reasoning
- OPTOE (Optics and Electromagnetic Waves): Physics concepts related to optics and electromagnetic phenomena
- PRST2 (Probability and Statistics 2): Advanced probability and statistical analysis
- POO (Object-Oriented Programming): Introduction to object-oriented programming concepts and practices
- ANA4 (Mathematical Analysis 4): Advanced mathematical analysis techniques
- SINF (Information Systems): Introduction to information systems design and management
- PRJP (Team Project): Collaborative project work focusing on IoT, web development, or mobile development

### PHASE 2: THREE YEARS ENGINEERING CYCLE

The second-cycle program lasts three years (six semesters), across all specialties:
- First year (1CS): Common core
