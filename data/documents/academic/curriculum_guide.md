# ESI Curriculum Guide

## The 5-Year Engineering Program at École Nationale Supérieure d'Informatique (ESI)

**Location:** Oued Smar, Algiers

The ESI engineering program is organized over **5 years**:

- **2 years of preparatory cycle:** 1CP and 2CP
- **3 years of higher cycle:** 1CS, 2CS, and 3CS
- **Access to the higher cycle:** Internal competitive exam between cycles
- **Degree:** State Engineer degree

This guide focuses exclusively on the academic curriculum: the structure of the program year by year, specialization, graduation project, internships, and the pathway to doctoral studies.

---

# 1. Preparatory Cycle — 1CP and 2CP

The first two years form the **classes préparatoires (preparatory classes)**.

Their goal is to give every student a common scientific and technical foundation before entering the higher cycle.

## 1CP — First Preparatory Year

The first year establishes the foundations of:

- Mathematics
- Algorithmics
- Introductory programming
- Electronics
- Systems
- General computer science fundamentals

The program is intentionally broad, giving students exposure to subjects they will build on later.

### Internship

An **industrial internship — also called the discovery internship — is scheduled at the end of 1CP**, providing students with an early practical experience of the professional world. It is **mandatory** and typically lasts **1 to 2 weeks**.

## 2CP — Second Preparatory Year

The second year deepens the foundations introduced in 1CP, particularly in:

- Algorithmics
- Systems
- Electronics
- Networks
- Mathematics

It prepares students for the **internal competitive exam (concours)** giving access to the higher cycle (1CS).

### Access to the Higher Cycle

Admission works through two tracks:

1. **Sur titre:** Students ranked in roughly the top 80% of their class are admitted directly based on their academic record over the two preparatory years.
2. **Sur épreuve:** Students outside the top 80% must take the written entrance exam and compete for the remaining places.

For example, during the **2025–2026 session**:

- 310 places were open
- 297 candidates were admitted
  - 248 admitted **sur titre**
  - 49 admitted **sur épreuve**

Exact terms, proportions, and numbers can change each year, so official announcements should always be checked.

The exam may also be open, under eligibility conditions, to students from other schools, universities, or university centers in the same field.

---

## Grading Rule — Note Éliminatoire

The elimination threshold applies throughout the curriculum, starting from **1CP**.

Each module has a **note éliminatoire**, meaning a minimum grade below which the module is failed.

It is **not a fixed number such as 10/20**. According to this guide, it is set at approximately:

> **60% (0.6×) of the class average for the relevant module and exam period.**

A compensation mechanism (**rachat**) can still allow a student below the threshold to validate the year, according to ESI's academic regulations.

### Key Point

**1CP and 2CP are not specialized years.** They build a common, rigorous foundation and prepare students for the internal exam determining access to the higher cycle.

---

# 2. Higher Cycle — Year 1 (1CS)

Once admitted through the competitive exam, students enter the **three-year higher cycle (cycle supérieur)**.

The first year, **1CS**, is a shared common-core year.

All students admitted to the higher cycle follow the same program, regardless of the specialization they will choose later.

### 1CS Characteristics

- Common-core coursework for all higher-cycle students
- Advanced continuation of the foundations acquired during the preparatory cycle
- A **mandatory company internship of 4 to 6 weeks**
- Internship takes place during 1CS
- 1CS class ranking contributes to determining which specialization a student can join

The ranking is considered together with the **number of available places** in each specialization.

> **Important:** The 1CS company internship is mandatory and lasts 4 to 6 weeks.

---

# 3. Higher Cycle — Years 2–3 (2CS / 3CS)

After the common-core year, students choose one of **four specializations** for the final two years of the engineering program.

The choice depends on:

- Student ranking
- Number of available places in each specialization

## The Four Specializations

| Code | Full Name | Main Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **SIQ** | Systèmes Informatiques (Computer Systems) | Computer systems engineering: architecture, operating systems, low-level and systems-oriented computing |
| **SIT** | Systèmes d'Information et Technologies (Information Systems & Technologies) | Design, integration and management of information systems and enterprise technologies |
| **SIL** | Systèmes Informatiques et Logiciels (Computer & Software Systems) | Software engineering, design, development and engineering of large-scale software systems |
| **SID** | Systèmes Intelligents et Données (Intelligent Systems & Data) | Artificial intelligence, data science and intelligent systems |

The two specialized years combine:

- Advanced theoretical coursework
- Project-based work
- Applied work specific to each specialization

They prepare students for both industry roles and, for students interested in research, further academic work.

> **Note:** Specialization names and scope reflect the four tracks currently listed by ESI. Exact course content within each specialization should be confirmed with the school's academic department because programs may be periodically updated.

---

# 4. Final Year — Graduation Project (PFE)

The final year of the specialization track, at the end of the 5-year program, is capped by the:

**Projet de Fin d'Études (PFE)**

The PFE is the graduation / end-of-studies project and represents the culminating academic work of the engineering program.

## PFE Characteristics

- Conducted during the final year of the higher cycle
- Takes place after the specialization coursework
- Consolidates knowledge acquired throughout the preparatory and specialization years
- Takes the form of a substantial project or dissertation (**mémoire de fin d'études**)
- Often relates to the student's specialization: **SIQ, SIT, SIL, or SID**
- May involve:
  - An external company
  - A research laboratory
  - An independent project

### Degree Requirement

**Successful completion of the PFE is required to obtain the State Engineer degree.**

---

# 5. Internship Timeline Across the Program

Practical, company-facing experience is integrated into the curriculum at several stages.

| Stage | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Industrial internship (discovery internship)** | End of 1CP | Mandatory, 1 to 2 weeks — first hands-on exposure to a professional environment |
| **Company internship** | During 1CS | Mandatory, 4 to 6 weeks in a company |
| **Graduation project (PFE)** | Final year (3CS) | Capstone project / dissertation closing the engineering program |

---

# 6. Beyond the Engineering Degree — Doctoral Studies

ESI is officially accredited to deliver **doctoral training**, making the school both an engineering institution and a research hub in computing and digital technologies.

## Doctoral Pathway

Graduates interested in academic research can pursue a **doctorate (PhD) at ESI** after completing the engineering degree.

Doctoral research is hosted within the school's **two research laboratories**, which bring together most of ESI's teacher-researchers.

### Research Areas

The guide identifies research areas including:

- Embedded systems
- Robotics
- Artificial intelligence
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Data science
- Medical image processing
- Advanced databases
- Knowledge engineering
- Optimization

### Discovering Research Early

Regularly organized **thesis and magister defenses** at the school can serve as an entry point for students who want to discover research as early as the preparatory or higher cycle.
