# ESI Specialities Guide

## The Four Engineering Majors at École Supérieure d'Informatique (ESI)

During the two years in major (4th and 5th year) of the engineering cycle, ESI offers students four majors:

- **SIT** — Information and Technology Systems
- **SIQ** — Informatics Systems
- **SIL** — Software Engineering Systems
- **SID** — Intelligent Systems and Data

Students choose one of the majors by the end of the first year of the second cycle (1CS), and are then oriented based on their marks obtained during the academic year and the number of available seats in each speciality.

Each major is organized over two semesters of the second-cycle year (2CS) and combines core computer science foundations with a specialized focus area.

---

# 1. SIT — Information and Technology Systems

SIT is a generalist engineering program in computer science with a focus on the field of information systems.

This training equips students with broad knowledge and diverse skills related to the various professions in computer science, as well as solid expertise in information systems and technologies, including:

- IS architectures
- Decision-support information systems
- ERP
- IS urbanization
- Quality assurance

Graduates of this speciality become designers and developers capable of understanding the engineering of any computer system. They can also:

- Analyze the needs of future users
- Align a company's information system with its strategic objectives
- Suggest suitable organizational structures
- Manage IT projects

- **TICO** — TIC en Organisation

# 5. How to Choose Your Speciality

The choice of major is made at the end of **1CS** and is influenced by both:

1. Personal preference
2. Student ranking, since seats per speciality are limited

### Practical Points

Look at the modules of each speciality and imagine which ones you would enjoy working on for a full year.

### SIT

Suits students drawn to:

- Enterprise IT
- Information system architecture
- Organizational aspects
- Business aspects

### SIQ

Suits students who enjoy:

- Systems
- Networks
- Security
- Infrastructure-level engineering

### SIL

Suits students who enjoy building software products end-to-end, especially:

- Web development
- Mobile development
- Application development

### SID

Suits students interested in:

- Mathematics
- Statistics
- Data
- Artificial intelligence

### Orientation and Seat Availability

Since orientation depends on ranking and seat availability, it is wise to have:

- A first choice
- A realistic backup choice
