# ESI Modules Guide

## Modules, Descriptions and Coefficients — All Years & Specialities

This guide covers the ESI engineering curriculum for **1CP, 2CP, 1CS**, and the four **2CS specialities: SID, SIT, SIL and SIQ**.

Each module includes:
- **Code**
- **Name**
- **Domain (DDC)**
- **Coefficient**
- A short, approximately two-line explanation to help students understand what the module is about.

> **Important:** The short explanations below are practical descriptions based on the module names and the domains provided in the source guide. They are intended to clarify the subject, not to replace ESI's official course syllabus.

### Domains (DDC)

- **Mathematics for Engineers** — mathematical foundations used throughout computer science and engineering.
- **Software Engineering** — programming, software design, development and engineering practices.
- **Infrastructure & Networks** — systems, networks, cloud, security and computing infrastructure.
- **Information Systems Engineering** — information systems, databases, enterprise technologies and organizational IT.
- **Data Science & AI** — data analysis, machine learning, optimization and intelligent systems.
- **SHSE** — Human, Social & Entrepreneurial Sciences, covering communication, organizations, management and entrepreneurship.

### Coefficients

The coefficient indicates the weight of a module in the semester/annual average. A higher coefficient means that the module has a greater impact on the overall average.

---

# 9. How to Read the Coefficients

The coefficient represents the module's weight in the average.

For example:

- A module with **coefficient 5** has a larger impact on the average than a module with **coefficient 2**.
- A module with **coefficient 1** has a comparatively smaller impact.
- High-coefficient modules therefore generally require more attention when planning study time.
