# El Alia University Residence for Female Students

## 1. General Information

El Alia University Residence for Female Students is a university residence located near the **École Nationale Supérieure d’Informatique (ESI)** in Algiers.

The residence mainly accommodates female students, including students from ESI, as well as students from other higher education institutions, including **École Polytechnique** and **EPAU**.

## 2. Rooms and Accommodation Structure

The residence is relatively spacious and has several green areas.

Rooms are generally shared by **two female students**.

The accommodation is organized into groups of four rooms located around a shared hallway or floor area. These four rooms share a common bathroom and sanitary facilities. This arrangement gives residents the feeling of living in a small independent housing unit.

### Individual Rooms

An individual room may be assigned in certain situations:

- Students with a relevant and properly documented **medical file** may, depending on the applicable conditions and room availability, receive an individual room.
- Without a medical file, an individual room may become available from the **third year of study**, depending on the residence's rules and room availability.

The allocation of individual rooms depends on the residence's current policies and the number of available rooms.

## 3. Water Availability

Water availability at the residence may be interrupted during certain periods of the day.

The reported schedule is:

- **Water available:** approximately from **6:00 AM to 10:00 AM**
- **Water unavailable:** approximately from **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM**
- **Water available:** approximately from **4:00 PM to 10:00 PM**
- **Water unavailable:** approximately from **10:00 PM to 6:00 AM**

Students should take these schedules into consideration, particularly for **showers, washing clothes, cleaning and other daily activities**.

These hours are approximate and may change depending on the residence's water supply and current conditions.

## 4. Study Room and Internet Access

The residence has a study room equipped with a **fiber-optic Internet connection**.

The study room is often crowded and can fill up quickly. Students are therefore advised to arrive early if they want to find a place.

## 5. Shops and Services

The residence provides several nearby services and shops for students' daily needs.

### Taxiphone and Kiosk

There is a taxiphone kiosk inside the residence. The kiosk also sells some stationery and bookstore-related items.

### Grocery Store

A small grocery store is available inside the residence, providing basic products and everyday necessities.

The stated opening hours are generally from **8:00 AM to midnight (12:00 AM)**.

## 6. Leisure Facilities

The residence has a leisure room where students can relax and participate in recreational activities.

The facilities include:

- a billiard table;
- a table-tennis table;
- a foosball table.

The residence also has a **foyer** offering light food and refreshments, including:

- fast food;
- tea;
- pastries;
- other consumer products.

## 7. Gym and Sports Activities

A small gym is available inside the residence. It is not particularly well equipped, but it allows students to perform basic physical exercises.

Collective sports activities may also be organized. The residence has a **handball team**, and volleyball activities may occasionally be organized.

## 8. Students Living in the Residence

El Alia Residence is not exclusively reserved for ESI students.

Students from several higher education institutions live there, including:

- **École Nationale Supérieure d’Informatique (ESI)**
- **École Polytechnique**
- **EPAU**
- other higher education institutions
