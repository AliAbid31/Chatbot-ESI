# Bouraoui Amar University Residence for Male Students

## 1. General Information

The **Bouraoui Amar University Residence for Male Students** is located in **El-Harrach**, approximately **4 km from the École Nationale Supérieure d’Informatique (ESI) in Algiers**.

The residence is exclusively reserved for **male students enrolled in higher education institutions and grandes écoles**.

## 2. Rooms and Accommodation Structure

The residence consists of several accommodation blocks.

### Double Rooms

Double rooms normally accommodate **two students**.

They are located in the following blocks:

- **Block K**
- **Block L**
- **Block M**
- **Block N** — currently under reconstruction
- **Block P** — currently under reconstruction

### Individual Rooms

Individual rooms are located in:

- **Block A**
- **Block C**
- **Block D**
- **Block B** — currently under reconstruction

### Block Z

**Block Z is currently closed.**

## 3. Room Assignment Rules

Room assignments depend on the student's level of study.

### Preparatory Cycle

Students in the **1st and 2nd years of the preparatory cycle** are normally assigned to double rooms in:

- **Block K**
- **Block L**
- **Block M**

### Higher Cycle

Students in the **higher cycle** are normally assigned to individual rooms in:

- **Block A**
- **Block C**
- **Block D**

### Exceptions for Preparatory-Cycle Students

A preparatory-cycle student who wishes to obtain an individual room may, according to the information provided:

1. Submit a request accompanied by a **medical file**.
2. Be associated with a student who **freezes or postpones their academic year**, allowing the student to occupy a double room alone.

Individual-room exceptions are **not guaranteed** and depend on the applicable administrative rules and circumstances.

### General Quality

The general quality of the **rooms, sanitary facilities and showers** is described as **modest but functional**.

## 4. Restaurant, Showers and Mosque

### University Restaurant

The university restaurant provides meals to students.

The quality of the food is described as **satisfactory**.

### Meal Schedule

| Meal | Schedule |
|---|---|
| **Breakfast** | 06:00 – 07:00 |
| **Lunch** | 12:00 – 14:00 |
| **Dinner** | 18:00 – 20:00 |

These schedules are indicative and may vary depending on the residence's administration.

### Showers

The showers are open **every day**, with schedules that may vary depending on the period.

The usual time slots are:

- **Morning:** 06:00 – 10:00
- **Evening:** 18:00 – 22:00

### Mosque

The residence has a **mosque** that is open for:

- the five daily prayers;
- Friday prayer (**Joumouaa**);
- **Tarawih** prayers.

The mosque also contains a **quiet library**, although the library is not particularly spacious.

## 5. Study Room and Internet Access

The residence provides:

- a **study room**;
- **Internet access**.

The Internet connection is described as having **average and basic performance**.

## 6. Shops and Services

### Mini-Grocery Store

A **mini-grocery store** is located inside the university residence.

It provides students with basic necessities and everyday products.

### Opening Hours

The usual opening hours are:

- **07:00 – 02:00**
- During examination periods, opening may be extended until **05:00**.

### Kiosk

There is **no kiosk inside the residence**.

However, a kiosk is located **immediately outside the residence**, near the main entrance.

## 7. Leisure Facilities

The residence has a **games room** equipped with:

- **2 foosball tables**
- **2 billiard tables**
- **1 table-tennis table**
- **1 Smart TV**
- Seating areas, including spaces where students can watch football matches.

### Foyer

The foyer includes:

- a **cafeteria**;
- a **fast-food service**.

According to the information provided, the quality of the food available outside the residence, near the main entrance, is generally considered better than the food available inside the residence.

## 8. Gym and Sports Facilities

The residence has several sports facilities, including:

- a **weight-training room**;
- a **large artificial-turf stadium**;
- a **calisthenics area**;
- a **covered multi-sports hall**.

According to the information provided, there are **no structured sports associations or organized sports clubs** within the residence.

## 9. Students Living in the Residence

Access to the residence is restricted to **male students** from the higher education institutions concerned.

Students from the following schools may be accommodated there:

1. **École Nationale Supérieure d’Informatique (ESI)**
2. **École Nationale Polytechnique (ENP)**
3. **École Polytechnique d’Architecture et d’Urbanisme (EPAU)**
4. **École Nationale Supérieure Agronomique (ENSA)**
5. **École Supérieure des Sciences de l’Alimentation et des Industries Agroalimentaires (ESSAIA)**
